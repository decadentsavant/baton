BATON

A hello from a stranger, in your bar.
Baton puts a hand in your bar. Click it, and somewhere in the world another Omarchy user's bar pulses. They can wave back. One bit out, one bit back.
★
What it does
👋

Wave

One click sends a hello to a random Omarch. No message to write.

🌍

See where it came from

“Someone in Poland.” A country, never a city, never a name.

🏅

Catch a baton

Some waves carry one. It remembers only hops, age, and countries.

🔒

Nothing to sign up for

No accounts, no feeds, no history. Just a hello.
